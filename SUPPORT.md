# Support

For community help, use the repository's issue tracker when it is available. Include a reproducible scenario, relevant configuration with secrets removed, FIX version and software version. Never post real credentials, private keys, customer data or unredacted FIX messages. Community support is offered on a best-effort basis.

For suspected vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than opening a public issue.

Informed Technology Business Solutions LLC (ITBS) offers optional paid help with installation, configuration, FIX counterparty integration, certification testing, troubleshooting and custom engineering. [Contact ITBS](https://www.itbsllc.com/). Paid services are not required to use the software.
